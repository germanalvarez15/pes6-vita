PES6 PS Vita — Stadiums & Commentary Helper

Convert stadiums and verify commentary banks directly in your browser — no installation required.

🔒 100% private & local: everything runs inside your own browser. Your ISO and files are never uploaded to any server.

STEP 1 Select your PSP pes6.iso

Use your own European copy of Pro Evolution Soccer 6 for PSP (ULES00476). All 38 PS2 stadiums are already inside — no extra files or PS2 disc required.

STEP 2 Choose which stadiums to convert

Every stadium includes Day & Night lighting, 3D goalposts, pitch-side adboards, and all 3 crowd densities (Low, Normal, and High).

Import stadiums from community PES6 PS2 patches

Convert custom stadiums made for PES6 on PS2 and automatically verify that they fit PS Vita memory limits before copying them to your console.

✅ Supported: patched PES6 PS2 ISO, PS2 0_TEXT.AFS, or PS2 stadium .bin ❌ Not supported: PES6 PC stadiums (Kitserver / Stadium Server)
ℹ️ First load your PSP pes6.iso in the 38 Official Stadiums tab (used only as a template for 3D goalposts and pitch adboards).

Verify or extract commentary (Stock & Community Patches)

Use stock PES6 PS2 commentary or community commentary packs made for PES6. If you have a PES6 PS2 ISO, you can also extract s_sound.afs / e_sound.afs directly here without 7-Zip.

✅ Supported: PES6 S_SOUND.AFS / E_SOUND.AFS (PS2 or PC) in CRI ADX format ❌ Not supported: PES 2008/2009/2010+, loose MP3/WAV, or PSP ISO

📁 Where to copy each file on your PS Vita (with VitaShell)

Connect your PS Vita to your PC over USB or FTP in VitaShell and place the files like this:

ux0:data/pes6vita/ ├── pes6.iso ← Your European PES6 PSP ISO (ULES00476) ├── audio/ │ ├── e_sound.afs ← English commentary (PS2 stock or PES6 patch, optional) │ └── s_sound.afs ← Spanish commentary (PS2 stock or PES6 patch, optional) └── stadiums/ ├── bernabeu.bin ← Converted stadiums from this page ├── bombonera.bin ├── camp_nou.bin └── ... (up to all 38 stadiums)

🎮 In-game: before kick-off open Match Settings → Stadium and Attendance (No / Low / Normal / High). Leave Stadium on Random to automatically match the home team's stadium.

This project does not include or distribute Konami game data. You need your own legal copies of the game.
