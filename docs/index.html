PES6 PS Vita — Patch Creator, Stadiums & Commentary Helper

Build full patches (teams, squads, player faces & crests), convert PS2 stadiums, and verify commentary banks 100% locally in your browser.

🔒 100% private & local: everything runs inside your own browser. Your ISO and files are never uploaded to any server.

STEP 1 Select your PSP pes6.iso

Use your own European copy of Pro Evolution Soccer 6 for PSP (ULES00476). All 38 PS2 stadiums are already inside — no extra files or PS2 disc required.

STEP 2 Choose which stadiums to convert

Every stadium includes Day & Night lighting, 3D goalposts, pitch-side adboards, and all 3 crowd densities (Low, Normal, and High).

Import stadiums from community PES6 PS2 patches

Convert custom stadiums made for PES6 on PS2 and automatically verify that they fit PS Vita memory limits before copying them to your console.

✅ Supported: patched PES6 PS2 ISO, PS2 0_TEXT.AFS, or PS2 stadium .bin ❌ Not supported: PES6 PC stadiums (Kitserver / Stadium Server)
ℹ️ First load your PSP pes6.iso in the 38 Official Stadiums tab (used only as a template for 3D goalposts and pitch adboards).

Verify or extract commentary (Stock & Community Patches)

Use stock PES6 PS2 commentary or community commentary packs made for PES6. If you have a PES6 PS2 ISO, you can also extract s_sound.afs / e_sound.afs directly here without 7-Zip.

✅ Supported: PES6 S_SOUND.AFS / E_SOUND.AFS (PS2 or PC) in CRI ADX format ❌ Not supported: PES 2008/2009/2010+, loose MP3/WAV, or PSP ISO

STEP 1 Patch Info (mod.json)

Create a custom patch for PS Vita. Once copied to ux0:data/pes6vita/mods/<id>/, you can select it from the SETTINGS button on the game's LiveArea screen (each patch keeps its own separate saves).

STEP 2 Teams, Squads & Players (Option File + Visual Editor)

Upload a PES6 PC Option File (KONAMI-WIN32PES6OPT, automatically decrypted and converted to PSP layout) or a PSP DATA.BIN. You can then inspect and edit leagues, clubs, custom PNG emblems, squads, and player stats directly below.

STEP 3 Player Faces (PC Faceserver Batch or Loose Images)

Drop a folder or selection of files from a PC patch's kitserver/GDB/faces/ (including map.txt + face .png / .bin files), or drop files named by player ID (e.g. 1340.png, 1340.bin). Every face is landmark-warped to the PSP's 32×64 head UV layout.

STEP 4 Full League Crest Bank from a PC Patch (Optional)

If you have a full PES6 PC patch's dat/0_text.afs (or extracted member 536 .flg), drop it here to convert all 169 club crests & national flags into PSP slots 2347.bin (32×32) and 2350.bin (64×64). Requires loading your PSP pes6.iso in Tab 1 first.

STEP 5 Build & Download Patch for PS Vita

Add an Option File, player faces, or a crest bank above, then click below to download the ready-to-install PS Vita patch ZIP.

📁 Where to copy each file on your PS Vita (with VitaShell)

Connect your PS Vita to your PC over USB or FTP in VitaShell and place the files like this:

ux0:data/pes6vita/ ├── pes6.iso ← Your European PES6 PSP ISO (ULES00476) ├── audio/ │ ├── e_sound.afs ← English commentary (PS2 stock or PES6 patch, optional) │ └── s_sound.afs ← Spanish commentary (PS2 stock or PES6 patch, optional) ├── stadiums/ │ ├── bernabeu.bin ← Converted stadiums from this page │ ├── bombonera.bin │ └── ... (up to all 38 stadiums) └── mods/ └── my-pes6-patch/ ← Unzipped patch from the Patch Creator tab ├── mod.json ├── save/ULES004760013000/{DATA.BIN, PARAM.BIN} ├── faces/{index.txt, <id>.pam} └── afs/0_text.afs/{2347.bin, 2350.bin}

🎮 Selecting a patch on PS Vita: tap SETTINGS on the game's LiveArea screen before starting (or hold L at boot) and change Game Patch / Mod to your patch.
